Make your listing useful

Use a specific name

Describe the product or animal clearly, including the variety or model where relevant.

Give an accurate price and unit

State whether the price is per kilogram, bag, piece, animal or complete item.

Include the location

A county or collection area helps buyers understand transport needs. Avoid posting private home details in a public listing.

Be clear about availability

When live selling launches, keep quantities and collection times up to date.
