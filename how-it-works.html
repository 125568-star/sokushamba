NUNUA. UZA. LIMA.

A market that brings people together.

SokoShamba is being built to connect Kenyan farmers directly with buyers. Here is how to explore the concept and prepare for the live marketplace.

For buyers

  1. Find a product

    Visit Buy and search by product or location, or choose a category to narrow your search.

  2. Read the listing

    Open the product page to review the price, selling unit, location and details to confirm.

  3. Agree on the details

    When live sellers are connected, confirm availability, quality, the total price and collection or delivery directly with the seller.

Explore listings

For farmers and sellers

  1. Prepare a clear listing

    Use the Sell page to enter the product name, category, price, unit and location.

  2. Save your draft

    The current concept saves your draft on this device. You can review it on the Saved Drafts page.

  3. Connect with buyers

    Public listing and enquiries are planned for the live marketplace. They are not available in this preview.

Prepare a listing

Keep each transaction clear

For any future trade, inspect products where practical, confirm who you are dealing with and agree on payment and transport terms. A listing alone does not verify the seller or guarantee product quality.

What works in this concept?

You can browse sample listings, search the marketplace, open category and product pages, and save drafts locally. Accounts, seller contact, ordering, payments and delivery booking are not connected yet.
