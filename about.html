A place to discover agricultural products and build direct connections between the people who grow, raise, supply and buy them.

The idea behind SokoShamba

Farmers need a way to show buyers what they have available. Buyers need useful information about products, prices and locations. SokoShamba brings those details into one agricultural marketplace.

The concept includes crops, livestock, farm inputs, machinery, planting materials, tools, land and services. It is designed for both small producers and businesses serving agriculture.

Nunua. Uza. Lima.

Buy. Sell. Farm. Those three actions sit at the centre of the marketplace: helping people find what they need, reach potential buyers and keep farming moving.
